Process X-ray · tested on real data

Your systems already know where the time goes. We show you.

A Process X-ray is a two-week analysis of the activity log your business systems already keep. Like an X-ray, it shows what's inside without opening anything up: no new software and no access to your systems, just one data export.

You send
One export from your ERP, ticketing, order or claims system, with four columns: case ID step timestamp person or team
We take
Two weeks, at a fixed price agreed up front. We sign a data processing agreement before you send anything.
You get
Your real process map, where the time goes, the bottlenecks and the fixes worth making first, with estimated impact, plus a walkthrough with your team.

Here's what an X-ray found in 4,580 real support tickets from a public dataset:

Where 40 days of ticket time actually went4,580 tickets · 2010–2014
  • Waiting window after the fix72%
  • Hands-on handling11%
  • Queue before pickup9%
  • Parked in "Wait" status8%
  • Escalated to development1%

Tickets took 40 days to close but only 6 to fix. Most of the difference was a fixed confirmation window, not work. Public data from an Italian software company's IT help desk; findings are associations, not proof of cause.

Questions

What people usually ask us.

Something else? Write to hello@goldenda.com.

We're not an AI company. Is this for us?

Yes, that's exactly who it's for. You don't need data scientists or new software. If your team repeats the same orders, emails, documents or process work every week, we can usually tell in one 30-minute call whether it's worth automating.

What happens on the free 30-minute call?

You walk us through one workflow that takes up your team's time. We ask a few questions about volume, systems and data, and tell you honestly whether AI can help, what a pilot would look like and what it would involve. No slides and no obligation.

What does a pilot cost?

A pilot is a fixed price agreed up front, scoped to one workflow and two to three weeks of work. We give you the number after the free call, once we know the size of the problem, and you decide whether to go ahead.

What happens to our data?

We sign a data processing agreement before we touch anything, keep processing inside the EU where possible, delete working copies when the project ends, and never use your data to train models for other clients.

Can't we just use ChatGPT or Copilot?

For one person's one-off tasks, often yes. But a chat window doesn't read your inbox, enter orders in your ERP or run on its own, and nobody measures whether its answers are right. We build the system around the model: connected to your tools, tested on your data, covered by a data processing agreement, and maintained by us. If a chat assistant is all you need, we'll tell you on the first call.

What exactly is a Process X-ray?

A two-week, fixed-price analysis of the activity log one of your business systems already keeps. We call it an X-ray because it shows what happens inside a process without changing anything. You get a map of how cases really flow, where time is lost, who is overloaded, and the fixes worth making first. All we need is one export with four columns: case ID, step, timestamp and person or team. We never need access to your systems.

Will it replace our control system?

No. For industrial processes, the AI sits on top of your existing PID and SCADA control and suggests or sets better setpoints within limits you define. It's tested in simulation on your historical data first, and your operators can always override it.

You're a young company. Why should we trust you?

Fair question. That's why we start with a small, fixed-price pilot and show measured results before you commit to more. You work directly with the two engineers who build your system, everything is covered by a written agreement, and you can verify our company registration on virk.dk.
