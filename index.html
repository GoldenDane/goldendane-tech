Process X-ray · tested on real data

Your systems already know where the time goes. We show you.

A Process X-ray is a two-week analysis of the activity log your business systems already keep. Like an X-ray, it lets you see inside without opening anything up: no new software and no access to your systems, just one data export. You get a map of how work really flows, and exactly where cases wait, loop or bounce between people.

You send
One export from your ERP, ticketing, order or claims system, with four columns: case ID step timestamp person or team
We take
Two weeks, at a fixed price agreed up front. We sign a data processing agreement before you send anything.
You get
A report with your process map, where the time goes, the bottlenecks, and the top fixes with estimated impact, plus a walkthrough with your team.

Here's what an X-ray found in 4,580 real support tickets from a public dataset:

  • Waiting window after the fix72%

Tickets took 40 days to close but only 6 to fix. Most of the difference was a fixed confirmation window, not work. Public data from an Italian software company's IT help desk; findings are associations, not proof of cause.

Questions

What people usually ask us.

We're not an AI company. Is this for us?

Yes, that's who it's for. You don't need data scientists or new software. If your team repeats the same document or process work every week, we can usually tell in one 30-minute call whether it's worth automating.

What exactly is a Process X-ray?

A two-week, fixed-price analysis of the activity log one of your business systems already keeps. It has nothing to do with medical imaging: we call it an X-ray because it shows what happens inside a process without changing anything. You get a map of how cases really flow, where time is lost, who is overloaded, and the fixes worth making first. See an example report.

What do you need from us for a Process X-ray?

One export from the system where the work happens (ERP, ticketing, order or claims system) with four columns: case ID, step, timestamp and person or team. Usually a few months of history. We never need access to your systems, and we sign a data processing agreement before you send anything.

What does a pilot cost?

A Prove pilot is a fixed price agreed up front, scoped to one workflow and two to three weeks of work. We'll give you the number after the free discovery call, once we know the size of the problem.

What happens to our data?

We agree a data processing agreement before we touch anything, keep processing inside the EU where possible, delete working copies when the project ends, and never use your data to train models for other clients.

Will it replace our control system?

No. For process work, the AI sits on top of your existing PID and SCADA control and suggests or sets better setpoints within limits you define. It's tested in simulation on your historical data first, and your operators can always override it.

You're a young company. Why should we trust you?

Fair question. That's why we start with a small, fixed-price pilot and show measured results before you commit to more. You also work directly with the two engineers who build your system.
